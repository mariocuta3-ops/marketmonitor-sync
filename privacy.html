Privacy Policy

MarketMonitor Sync is a private, non-commercial application.

The application accesses Google Drive using the drive.file permission to create, read and update its own market data files.

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's private server. Market data files are stored in the owner's Google Drive.

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Files may be accessed through other services explicitly connected and authorized by the owner.

Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
